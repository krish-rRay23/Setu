Evidence & benchmark methodology

Final held-out synthetic benchmark used to produce every number on this dashboard.

800total events
560clean / unambiguous
240ambiguous / conflicting
5unique ambiguous patterns
Classification accuracy / F1 is not used as a headline metric. The synthetic ambiguous set contains only ~5 unique signal patterns, so a 100% accuracy figure would not be a meaningful real-world claim. Setu is evaluated on measured economic and safety outcomes instead — net recovery, unsafe-action count, and false-retry rate.
